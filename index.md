## Two PM Paths

- Waterfall<!-- .element: style="font-size: 3rem;" -->

  - Linear (proposal → lit review → research → writing → defense)
  - Deadline driven
  - Inflexible

- Agile<!-- .element: style="font-size: 3rem;" -->
  - Iterative (work on what’s needed, in sprints)
  - Adaptive
  - Too flexible

---

![¿Por qué no los dos?](https://external-content.duckduckgo.com/iu/?u=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.tenor.com%2FBUtUo71HCgUAAAAM%2Fred89z.gif&f=1&nofb=1&ipt=5c349732566fa2d27814a0a2fbe9ecc4efb87f6ca872484698859e90b2e43bdd&ipo=images)
